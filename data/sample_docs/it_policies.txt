IT SECURITY POLICIES
Version: 5.2 | Last Updated: March 10, 2024 | Owner: Information Security Team

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. PURPOSE
==========
This policy protects Acme Corporation's information assets, systems, and data from
unauthorised access, misuse, or breach. All employees, contractors, and third-party
users with access to company systems must comply.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

2. PASSWORD REQUIREMENTS
=========================
Minimum password length: 14 characters
Complexity: At least 1 uppercase, 1 lowercase, 1 digit, 1 special character
Password reuse: Last 12 passwords may not be reused
Expiry: Passwords expire every 90 days (prompted automatically)
Password manager: 1Password is the approved corporate tool (licences provided)
  — Download from: https://portal.acmecorp.com → Software → 1Password

Never write passwords down or share them with anyone, including IT staff.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

3. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (MFA)
=====================================
MFA is mandatory for:
  • Corporate email (Microsoft 365)
  • VPN connections
  • All cloud SaaS applications in the approved app catalogue
  • The Employee Self-Service Portal

Approved MFA methods (in order of preference):
  1. Microsoft Authenticator app (push notification)
  2. Hardware security key (YubiKey — request from IT)
  3. TOTP authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy)

SMS-based MFA is NOT approved due to SIM-swap vulnerabilities.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

4. DEVICE POLICY
================
All devices accessing corporate data must:
  a) Be enrolled in Microsoft Intune (MDM)
  b) Run an approved, up-to-date OS
     — Windows 11 22H2+ | macOS 13+ | iOS 16+ | Android 12+
  c) Have disk encryption enabled (BitLocker / FileVault)
  d) Have corporate endpoint protection (Defender for Business) installed
  e) Apply security patches within 7 days of release

Personal devices are permitted under BYOD Policy (see Section 6).

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

5. DATA CLASSIFICATION
=======================
| Level          | Examples                          | Controls                |
|----------------|-----------------------------------|-------------------------|
| Public         | Marketing materials, job postings | None required           |
| Internal       | Policies, wikis, project docs     | Login required          |
| Confidential   | Customer data, financials, IP     | MFA + encryption        |
| Restricted     | PII, credentials, legal docs      | MFA + DLP + approval    |

Employees must only access data required for their role (least-privilege principle).

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

6. BYOD (BRING YOUR OWN DEVICE) POLICY
========================================
Personal devices may access corporate email and approved apps IF:
  • Enrolled in Intune Company Portal app
  • Corporate data kept in approved apps (Outlook, Teams, OneDrive)
  • Device PIN/biometric lock enabled
  • Remote wipe of corporate data is consented to

BYOD devices cannot access the development environment, financial systems, or
Restricted-classified data.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

7. ACCEPTABLE USE
==================
Permitted:
  • Use of company systems for legitimate business purposes
  • Limited personal use that does not impact productivity or security

Not permitted:
  • Downloading or installing unapproved software
  • Accessing torrent sites, adult content, or gambling sites
  • Using personal cloud storage (Google Drive, Dropbox) for company data
  • Circumventing security controls (VPN kill switches, firewalls)
  • Mining cryptocurrency on company resources

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

8. INCIDENT RESPONSE
====================
If you suspect a security incident (phishing, malware, unauthorised access):
  1. Do NOT click links or attachments if phishing is suspected
  2. Disconnect the affected device from the network
  3. Report IMMEDIATELY to: security@acmecorp.com | ext. 9911
  4. Preserve all evidence (do not restart or wipe the device)
  5. Follow instructions from the Security Operations team

Never attempt to investigate or remediate an incident yourself.

9. COMPLIANCE & CONSEQUENCES
=============================
Policy violations are subject to disciplinary action up to and including termination.
Serious violations may be referred to law enforcement.

Annual security awareness training is mandatory for all employees.
Completion deadline: 30 days from hire date; annually thereafter.
Training platform: https://training.acmecorp.com
