1. WELCOME
==========
Welcome to Acme Corporation! This handbook outlines your rights, responsibilities,
and the key policies that govern employment here. Please read it carefully and keep
it as a reference.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

2. CODE OF CONDUCT
==================
Every employee is expected to:
  • Act with integrity and treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect.
  • Avoid conflicts of interest and disclose any potential conflicts to HR.
  • Protect confidential company and customer information.
  • Comply with all applicable laws and regulations.
  • Report policy violations to ethics@acmecorp.com (anonymous reporting available).

Zero-tolerance behaviours include harassment, discrimination, fraud, and workplace violence.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

3. WORK HOURS & REMOTE WORK
============================
Standard hours: 9 AM – 5 PM local time (flexible with manager approval).
Core hours (must be available): 10 AM – 3 PM.

Remote work:
  • Up to 3 days/week remote is permitted for eligible roles.
  • Full-remote arrangements require written VP approval.
  • All remote work requires use of the company VPN.
  • Employees must be reachable via Slack/Teams during core hours.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

4. LEAVE ENTITLEMENTS
=====================
Annual (vacation) leave:
  • Years 0–2:  15 days / year
  • Years 3–5:  18 days / year
  • Years 6+:   22 days / year

Sick leave:        10 days / year (no carryover)
Personal days:      3 days / year
Parental leave:    16 weeks primary carer | 6 weeks secondary carer (paid)
Bereavement:        5 days for immediate family; 3 days extended family
Public holidays:   As mandated by your country/state of employment

To book leave: Workday → My Team → Time Off → Request Leave

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

5. PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
======================
  • Annual review: January each year (self-assessment + manager review)
  • Mid-year check-in: July (informal, not rated)
  • New hire 30/60/90-day check-ins during probation period
  • Ratings scale: Exceeds / Meets / Partially Meets / Does Not Meet expectations

Raises and bonuses are tied to performance rating and company results.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

6. LEARNING & DEVELOPMENT
==========================
  • Each employee has an annual L&D budget of $1,500 USD.
  • Access LinkedIn Learning, Pluralsight, and internal Acme Academy at no cost.
  • External training/conferences require manager approval before booking.
  • Reimbursement form: Workday → Expenses → Training & Development

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

7. BENEFITS SUMMARY
===================
  • Health insurance: Medical, Dental, Vision (details in Benefits Guide)
  • Retirement: 401(k) with 4% company match (vests after 1 year)
  • Life insurance: 2× annual salary, company-paid
  • Employee Assistance Programme (EAP): Free 24/7 confidential counselling
  • Home office stipend: $500 one-time for remote-eligible employees

Full benefits details: https://benefits.acmecorp.com

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

8. IT & EQUIPMENT
==================
  • Hardware is provided for all employees; personal devices need MDM enrolment.
  • Software requests: IT Self-Service portal → New Request → Software.
  • Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT Helpdesk within 2 hours.
