LEAVE & TIME-OFF POLICY
Version: 6.0 | Last Updated: January 1, 2024 | Owner: Human Resources

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. ANNUAL (VACATION) LEAVE
===========================
Leave accrues from the first day of employment.

| Service Length | Annual Entitlement |
|----------------|--------------------|
| 0 – 2 years    | 15 days            |
| 3 – 5 years    | 18 days            |
| 6+ years       | 22 days            |

Accrual: Leave accrues monthly (e.g., 15 days = 1.25 days/month).
Carryover: Up to 5 unused days may carry over to the next calendar year.
           Days beyond 5 are forfeited on January 1.
Payout: Accrued unused leave is paid out upon resignation or termination.

Minimum booking unit: Half-day (0.5 days).

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

2. SICK LEAVE
=============
Entitlement: 10 days per calendar year (non-accruing, front-loaded on January 1).
No carryover. Unused sick days are forfeited at year-end.

Extended illness:
  • After 5 consecutive sick days, a medical certificate is required.
  • After 10 days, short-term disability (STD) benefits may apply — contact HR.

New hires receive a pro-rated sick leave allocation based on start date.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

3. PERSONAL DAYS
================
Entitlement: 3 days per year (non-accruing, no carryover).
Use: No reason required; booked like annual leave but tracked separately.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

4. PARENTAL LEAVE
=================
Primary carer:  16 weeks fully paid
Secondary carer: 6 weeks fully paid

Eligibility: Employees with ≥ 6 months of service.
  • Covers birth, adoption, and fostering.
  • Must notify HR at least 8 weeks before leave is expected to begin.
  • Additional unpaid leave of up to 12 weeks may be requested.

Parental leave does not affect annual leave accrual or benefits.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

5. BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
====================
Immediate family (spouse, child, parent, sibling, grandparent): 5 days
Extended family (in-laws, aunts/uncles, cousins):               3 days
Close friends (at manager's discretion):                         1 day

Leave can be taken non-consecutively within 30 days of the bereavement.
Supporting documentation may be requested by HR.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

6. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
==================
Employees receive all statutory public holidays applicable to their country/state.
US employees receive 11 federal holidays + 2 company-designated "Recharge Days."

If a public holiday falls on a weekend, the preceding Friday or following Monday
is observed (unless business-critical).

Full public holiday calendar: https://hr.acmecorp.com/holidays

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

7. OTHER LEAVE TYPES
====================
Jury duty:       Fully paid for up to 15 days; notify HR immediately on summons.
Military leave:  As required by applicable law; contact HR for details.
Study/exam leave: Up to 5 days/year for approved qualification examinations.
Volunteer leave: 2 days/year for approved charitable activities.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

8. HOW TO REQUEST LEAVE
========================
  1. Discuss with your manager at least 2 weeks in advance (emergency leave excepted).
  2. Log in to Workday: Workday → My Team → Time Off → Request Leave
  3. Select leave type, dates, and add a note if desired.
  4. Your manager approves or declines within 2 business days.
  5. You will receive an email confirmation.

Team calendar visibility: Your leave is shown on the team calendar in Outlook/Teams
once approved.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

9. LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWOP)
============================
In exceptional circumstances unpaid leave may be granted for:
  — Extended personal or family matters
  — Career breaks (sabbaticals)
  — Voluntary public service

Maximum LWOP: 12 months. Requires VP + HR Director approval.
Benefits are suspended during LWOP periods exceeding 30 days.
