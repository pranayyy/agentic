EXPENSE & REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Version: 4.1 | Last Updated: January 2024 | Owner: Finance Department

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. OVERVIEW
===========
Acme Corporation reimburses employees for reasonable, pre-approved business expenses.
This policy defines what qualifies, spending limits, and how to submit claims.

All expenses must be submitted within 30 days of being incurred.
Late submissions (> 30 days) require Finance Director approval.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

2. APPROVED EXPENSE CATEGORIES
================================

2.1 Travel
----------
  • Flights: Economy class for journeys < 6 hours; Business permitted for 6+ hours
    (VP approval required for Business class)
  • Hotel: Up to $200 USD/night in standard markets; $300 in high-cost cities
    (NYC, SF, London, Tokyo, etc.) — book via the corporate travel portal
  • Ground transport: Uber/Lyft/taxi fully reimbursed; personal car at $0.67/mile (IRS 2024 rate)
  • Meals during travel: Up to $80 USD/day (no itemisation required below limit)
  • Per diem option available for international travel — contact Finance

2.2 Client Entertainment
------------------------
  • Meals with clients: Up to $120 per person (manager approval required)
  • Gifts to clients: Up to $50 per person per year (must be documented)
  • Events/tickets: Require VP approval; directly billed where possible

2.3 Office & Supplies
----------------------
  • Home office supplies: Up to $200/year without additional approval
  • Equipment (monitor, keyboard, etc.): Requires IT and manager approval;
    purchased through IT catalogue at portal.acmecorp.com → Hardware

2.4 Training & Conferences
---------------------------
  • Within L&D budget ($1,500/year per employee): No additional approval needed
  • Above L&D budget: Manager + HR approval required before spending
  • All training expenses submitted via Workday → Expenses → Training

2.5 Software & Subscriptions
------------------------------
  • Must be on the IT-approved software list
  • Individual subscriptions not on the approved list require IT + manager sign-off
  • Team/enterprise licences: Procured through IT; do not purchase independently

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

3. WHAT IS NOT REIMBURSED
==========================
  × Alcohol (unless part of an approved client entertainment meal;
    alcohol is capped at 20% of total meal cost)
  × Personal entertainment, fines, or traffic tickets
  × First-class flights or luxury hotel upgrades
  × Spouse/partner travel unless pre-approved by a VP
  × Personal care items, clothing, gym memberships
  × Expenses without receipts (receipts required for all items ≥ $25)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

4. SUBMITTING AN EXPENSE REPORT
================================
Step-by-step:
  1. Collect all receipts (photo/scan accepted; must be legible)
  2. Log in to Workday: https://acmecorp.workday.com
  3. Go to Expenses → Create Expense Report
  4. Select expense type, date, amount, currency, and purpose
  5. Attach receipt images
  6. Add business justification (one sentence minimum)
  7. Submit for manager approval
  8. Finance reviews after manager approval
  9. Reimbursement processed in next payroll cycle (biweekly)

Mobile app: Download Workday app → same process from your phone.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

5. CORPORATE CREDIT CARD (P-Card)
===================================
Senior employees (Grade 7+) may request a P-Card for frequent business expenses.
  • Request: IT Self-Service → Finance → P-Card Application
  • Monthly statement must be reconciled in Workday by the 5th of each month
  • Personal charges on P-Card result in immediate card suspension

P-Card limits: $2,000/transaction | $10,000/month (default)

6. COMPLIANCE
=============
Submitting fraudulent or inflated expenses is a terminable offence and may be
referred to law enforcement. Finance conducts random audits of expense reports.
