WELCOME TO ACME CORPORATION!
This guide walks you through everything you need to do in your first 30 days.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

DAY 1 CHECKLIST
================
□ Collect your laptop and access badge from Reception (Building A, ground floor)
□ Sign the Acceptable Use Policy and NDA (HR will send via DocuSign)
□ Attend IT orientation (9 AM, Room 204 — or virtual link sent by calendar)
□ Set up your email: firstname.lastname@acmecorp.com
□ Change your temporary password at https://passwords.acmecorp.com
□ Enrol in MFA at https://aka.ms/mfasetup
□ Install required software (IT Self-Service portal → New Hire Bundle)
□ Join your team Slack workspace and the #new-hires channel
□ Meet your buddy (assigned by your manager before Day 1)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WEEK 1 TASKS
=============
□ Complete mandatory HR training in Workday (Employee Handbook acknowledgement)
□ Complete IT Security Awareness training at https://training.acmecorp.com
  ⚠ Must be completed within 30 days of start date
□ Set up 1Password (corporate password manager — licence provided via IT)
□ Enrol your device in MDM (Intune):
     Windows: Company Portal app from Microsoft Store
     macOS: Company Portal from Self-Service Portal
□ Set up VPN access (see full VPN Setup Guide in the IT Wiki)
□ Schedule 1:1 meetings with key team members (your manager will share a list)
□ Review your role's 30/60/90-day goals with your manager

PAYROLL SETUP
=============
  1. Log in to Workday → Pay → Payment Elections
  2. Enter your bank details for direct deposit
  3. Payroll runs biweekly (every other Friday)
  4. Pay stubs available in Workday → Pay → Payslips
  5. Tax forms (W-4/state forms): Workday → Benefits & Pay → Tax Elections

Payroll questions: payroll@acmecorp.com | ext. 3100

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

BENEFITS ENROLMENT
==================
You have 30 days from your start date to enrol in benefits.
After 30 days, you must wait for the annual open-enrolment window.

  1. Log in to Workday → Benefits → Enrol in Benefits
  2. Review medical, dental, and vision plan options
  3. Nominate beneficiaries for life insurance and retirement

Benefits guide: https://benefits.acmecorp.com
Benefits questions: benefits@acmecorp.com | ext. 3200

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

30/60/90-DAY MILESTONES
=========================
Day 30:  Complete all mandatory training; HR check-in with your manager
Day 60:  Mid-probation review — discuss progress against role goals
Day 90:  End-of-probation review — formal performance conversation with manager
         (Probation confirmed or extended as needed)
